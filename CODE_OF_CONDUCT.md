# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors across backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements with evidence, acknowledge others' work, and accept constructive feedback.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate and documented privately.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact channel configured on the public repository. Do not post private information in an issue. Maintainers are subject to the same standards and should recuse themselves from reports involving their own conduct.

This policy is an original project policy. A public reporting contact must be configured before release.
